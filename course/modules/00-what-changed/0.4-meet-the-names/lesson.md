found: The plan from 0.3 is the cuPhoton pipeline: xRep aligns, xPois matches and subtracts, xFit measures what's left, xScan ranks it. A leftover dipole has two possible stories, and only a comparison with the other, unchanged sources tells them apart.

The static stars measure the pointing, the blur and the background for you, and whatever doesn't fit that shared pattern is a candidate.
